# Interview questionnaire

Ask one section at a time. Questions marked **Required** must be answered or explicitly marked TBD.

## 1. Initiative identity
- **Required:** What is the initiative name and one-sentence promise?
- Which portfolio, business unit, or product family owns it?
- Who is the executive audience and decision owner?
- What is the requested decision: approve, prioritize, fund, pilot, or stop?

## 2. Customer and problem
- **Required:** Who is the primary customer or user?
- **Required:** What recurring job or problem are they trying to solve?
- What is the current workflow and where are the largest delays, costs, or risks?
- What evidence supports the problem? Mark hypotheses clearly.

## 3. Outcome and behavior change
- **Required:** What measurable customer outcome should improve?
- Describe today's behavior and the intended future behavior.
- What is the shortest defensible value proposition?
- What should be the north-star metric?

## 4. Solution and differentiation
- **Required:** What experience or capability will be delivered?
- What is available now, what is next, and what is roadmap only?
- Why can your organization win? What is hard to copy?
- What is explicitly out of scope?

## 5. Portfolio and operating model
- Which products, platforms, teams, APIs, or channels are involved?
- What are the key dependencies and commercial gates?
- Who owns product, engineering, security, UX, go-to-market, and finance decisions?

## 6. Commercial model
- **Required:** How does the initiative grow revenue, retention, adoption, or efficiency?
- Who is the economic buyer and who are the champions, users, and approvers?
- What pricing or packaging hypothesis should be tested?
- Provide only supported scenario inputs: addressable cohort, attach/conversion assumptions, price, unit cost, and timing.

## 7. Roadmap and proof
- What can be delivered now, near term, medium term, and beyond?
- What evidence is required to pass each phase gate?
- What design-partner or pilot plan will prove value?

## 8. Risks, governance, and recommendation
- What are the top risks and mitigations?
- What trust, security, compliance, approval, or operational controls are required?
- What recommendation should the document make?
- What assumptions must be validated next?
